A note from the curator

The DS went on sale in North America on 21 November 2004 and in Japan on 2 December 2004, but Europe had to wait until 11 March 2005. It finished at around 154 million units, one of the best-selling consoles ever made. This collection follows the platform from Europe's side: the launch line-up, the June 2006 double of Dr Kawashima's Brain Training (9 June) and the slimmer DS Lite (23 June), the arrival of the DSi in April 2009, and a small shelf of DSiWare that closed to new purchases in Europe on 31 March 2017.

European release years and titles come first. Where North America or Japan differed in a way worth telling, the card says so: Project Rub was Feel the Magic, Pandora's Box was Diabolical Box, Style Boutique was Style Savvy. A few games reached Europe before anyone else, including Castlevania: Dawn of Sorrow, Fire Emblem: Shadow Dragon, Cooking Guide, Picross DS and Solatorobo. Others never arrived at all: Radiant Historia, Contra 4, 999, Retro Game Challenge and The Legendary Starfy are listed under their North American or Japanese years and say so, and Tomodachi Collection never left Japan. Because the DS has its own built-in screens, there is no PAL 50Hz conversion story to tell here.
