Card concept — rotated to match the "Collections" italic

Same background-motif idea as before, now angled. I measured the actual slant of the italic "Collections" wordmark pixel-by-pixel from your screenshot — both letterforms' stems line up at 14.6° off vertical, extremely consistently — and applied that exact same rotation to every card's background graphic below. The motif is each platform's own controller (or, for Game Boy Color, the handheld body itself), fading toward the card's center.

Things worth deciding once you've looked: how strong the fade should be (styles 1 vs 2, or something between); whether the motif should be each platform's controller (shown here) or something else per platform — a cartridge/disc silhouette, like last round's icon shapes, just blown up large; and whether the freed-up space should go to a bigger title (done here), a longer tagline, or an extra stat. The 14.6° rotation itself reads as a deliberate signature now, tying the card art back to your own wordmark rather than looking like a random tilt.
