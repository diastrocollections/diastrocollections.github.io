A field guide to twelve classic platforms

Diastro Gaming Collections

Pick a console. Each one opens onto its own Explorer's Collection — a hundred games spanning every genre the hardware ever touched, not just the titles that top a review-score spreadsheet. Twelve chapters apiece, fact-checked, searchable, built to be browsed rather than ranked. Five of the twelve — Mega Drive, NES, SNES, N64, and PS1 — are catalogued the European way, PAL names and years leading; the rest lead with North America.

The shelf

12 collections

Every collection here follows the same method: genre-by-genre research across enthusiast and critic sources (never just a Metacritic top-N), a curator's rationale per chapter, and an independent fact-check pass on every title's platform, release year, and developer before publishing. Twelve platforms deep — a solid start, not a hard stop. More consoles may join the shelf later.
