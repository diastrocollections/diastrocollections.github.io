A note from the curator

Every prior collection in this series used North American branding and release years as the default. This one flips that convention on purpose: it was called Mega Drive everywhere outside the United States, and this list uses the European name, the PAL box titles, and PAL release dates as the primary record — with the North American equivalent noted wherever a game actually shipped under a different name across the Atlantic.

That regional-naming quirk runs deeper on this platform than on most. A handful of games arrived in Europe with new titles entirely — a Contra game recast as Probotector with robot combatants standing in for its soldiers, a Turtles game rebranded Teenage Mutant Hero Turtles for the same censor-driven reasons, the action-RPG called Crusader of Centy in North America known here as Soleil. Where the PAL release never happened at all, that's flagged explicitly below rather than quietly defaulting back to an American date.

Scope is the base cartridge library only — Mega-CD and 32X add-on titles belong to a separate future collection. Twelve genre chapters sample the platform's full range, from Sega's own arcade pedigree to the distinctly British and French developer scene — Bitmap Brothers, Codemasters, Sensible Software, Delphine — that gave this console a flavor its American library never quite had.
