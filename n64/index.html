A note from the curator

The N64 is a console of trade-offs: Nintendo's choice of cartridges over CDs bought faster load times and killed piracy, but it also meant far less storage than Sony's rival — which shows up directly in this collection's RPG chapter, the smallest of the twelve at just six entries, a genuine reflection of how thin the genre's N64 output really was compared to the PlayStation's disc-fed library.

Release years follow this project's standing convention: European release year first where one exists, Japanese and/or North American year noted secondarily. The N64 also produced an unusually large crop of Japan-exclusive titles that never received any Western cartridge at all — several of them, like Sin and Punishment and Animal Forest, are now celebrated classics that the West only ever got to play through later re-releases on completely different hardware.

This build continues the leaner research process introduced with the SNES collection: well-established, unambiguous titles were drawn straight from existing knowledge rather than independently re-verified, and dedicated research went specifically toward the thirty games carrying a real regional-exclusivity claim, an unfamiliar developer credit, or a title that differs by region.

One near-miss worth noting: SimCity 2000 looked at first glance like it might repeat the NES collection's SimCity mistake, where a game was curated in before confirming it actually shipped. It checks out here — SimCity 2000 did release on N64, just as a Japan-exclusive cartridge with added mini-games, never officially reaching the West on this platform at all.
